# Credits and data sources

- **Elevation:** U.S. Geological Survey, 3D Elevation Program (3DEP). Public domain.
- **Trails, lifts, paths, buildings:** © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the
  [Open Database License](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
- **Roads, the railway, water, ponds, lookouts and place names:** also © OpenStreetMap contributors.
- **Weather:** [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), CC BY 4.0. The game asks only for the
  conditions at the mountain's coordinates.
- **Rendering:** [three.js](https://threejs.org), MIT License.
- **Sound:** made in the browser with Web Audio; there are no recordings.
- **Portraits and field-guide plates:** generated for this game with FLUX.1 [schnell] (Black
  Forest Labs) through [fal](https://fal.ai), from prompts in `tools/art/manifest.mjs`.
- **Lettering:** [Bebas Neue](https://github.com/dharmatype/Bebas-Neue) by Ryoichi Tsunekawa and
  [Caveat](https://github.com/googlefonts/caveat) by Impallari Type, both under the SIL Open
  Font License 1.1, bundled through [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org).

The people of the town in this game are invented, and so are their businesses' menus and their
opinions. Some buildings carry the names of real places in North Creek; nothing said or sold in
them here is theirs.
